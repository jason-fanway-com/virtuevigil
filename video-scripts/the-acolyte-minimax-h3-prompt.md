## CRITICAL PRODUCTION NOTES FOR MINIMAX H3

### Debra Ducane Character Consistency
- ALL appearances of Debra must match REF-1: same face, same hair, same skin tone, same build. This is a CONVINCING TALKING CHARACTER, not a generic AI woman.
- Her facial expressions should be natural and restrained — no exaggerated eyebrow raises, no cartoon double-takes. She's a journalist, not a TikTok reactor.
- Slight natural asymmetry in her face (one corner of mouth lifts slightly more, as real faces do).
- Her blink rate: approximately once every 4-6 seconds. Realistic, not robotic.
- Her head makes subtle micro-movements even when still — the way a real person's head does when they're concentrating on what they're saying.

### Lighting Continuity
- Scene 1: Rembrandt key (dramatic, forest environment)
- Scene 2: High-key from right (starfield rim light)
- Scene 5: Warm key from front-right (intimate, trust-building)
- Debra's skin tone must remain consistent across all lighting changes.

### Motion Design Principles
- All UI elements (cards, meters, bars) should have PHYSICS: they accelerate, decelerate, and land with weight. No linear motion. Use ease-out for arrivals, ease-in for departures.
- Trope cards rotate slightly during flight (3-15°), then snap to flat on landing.
- The Woke Meter liquid should feel viscous — thick, slow-moving, like real mercury or oil.
- Text animations should feel editorial: clean, fast, precise. Think Vox/Johnny Harris explainer videos, not PowerPoint.

### Color Palette (Strict)
- Background blacks: #0A0A0A (not pure #000000 — this preserves detail in dark areas)
- Gold: #C9A84C (all branding, accents, borders, the logo)
- Woke Red: #E53E3E (verdicts, woke score bars, severity indicators)
- Traditional Green: #38A169 (trad score bar only)
- White: #FFFFFF (primary text)
- Woke Trap Orange: #DD6B20 (not used in this video but reserved)

- 9:16 VERTICAL (1080×1920) — optimized for YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels
- Render at 30fps
- Final output: MP4, H.264, 1080×1920

### Audio Mix
