2022 was a year of whiplash at the box office. On one end, Top Gun: Maverick became the highest-grossing film of Tom Cruise's career and a cultural event that crossed every demographic line. On the other, Disney released Strange World, a $180 million animated feature that became one of the studio's most expensive flops in modern history. Between those poles sat 31 other films in the VirtueVigil database, each scored on our VVWS dual-metric system, and together they tell a story about what Hollywood thought audiences wanted and what audiences actually showed up for.

The VirtueVigil Woke Score (VVWS) measures progressive ideological content: identity politics, institutional distrust, progressive sexual framing, and systemic oppression narratives. The Traditional Score measures traditional values: courage, family, faith, sacrifice, moral clarity, and individual agency. The margin between them is what matters. A negative margin means the film pushes progressive ideology. A positive margin means it affirms traditional values. The size of the gap tells you how hard it pushes.

Of the 33 scored 2022 films, 9 lean woke, 1 lands dead even, and 23 lean traditional. That might sound like good news for viewers who prefer traditional entertainment, but the numbers are deceptive. The woke films of 2022 had enormous marketing budgets and the full backing of the awards-industrial complex. Glass Onion, which lands at -20.2 WOKE, was a major Netflix release positioned for awards season. Everything Everywhere All at Once, at a near-neutral +1.0 margin, won seven Academy Awards including Best Picture. The traditional films, meanwhile, were often dismissed by critics as lowbrow or jingoistic, even when they were objectively better made. The culture war is not between equals. The institutions are on one side.

Here is every 2022 film in the VirtueVigil database, ranked from most woke to most traditional. Every entry links to the full review with content warnings for parents and complete category breakdowns.

Strange World is the movie where Disney stopped pretending. Turning Red is technically brilliant and ideologically loaded. #5: All Quiet on the Western Front

Woke Score: 13.9 • Traditional Score: 9.0 • Verdict: BALANCED WOKE • Margin: -4.9

Genre: War / Drama | Platform: Netflix | Director: Edward Berger

Edward Berger's German-language adaptation of Remarque's 1929 novel is a technically brilliant film with a clear ideological agenda, and those two things are inseparable. The film adds a parallel storyline, absent from the novel, in which German officials negotiate the armistice while the generals cynically order one final assault. That addition transforms the story from a soldier's-eye view of war's horror into an argument about the moral bankruptcy of military institutions and national pride. The 13.9 woke score reflects institutional distrust and anti-war messaging elevated to thesis. The 9.0 traditional score reflects the genuine comradeship and sacrifice among the soldiers themselves. The film won four Academy Awards including Best International Feature and Best Cinematography. It deserves every technical award it received. It is also, unmistakably, a film that argues that patriotism is a lie that old men tell young men to get them killed.
