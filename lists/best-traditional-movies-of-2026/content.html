2026 has been a strange year at the movies. The box office rebounded. Franchises returned. Streaming continued its slow contraction. The ideology pipeline kept dutifully flowing. And yet, at the margins, a counter-current has been building all year.

That counter-current is tradition. It is not a genre. It is not a studio. It does not carry a label on the poster. It is something simpler: films made by people who either believed in something worth defending, or trusted their audience enough not to lecture them. Films that treat faith as real rather than as a pathology. Films that treat heroism as worth celebrating rather than deconstructing. Films that treat family, sacrifice, courage, and duty as worthy subjects rather than problems to be complicated.

VirtueVigil has reviewed 166 films released in 2026 with full VVWS scores. Of those, 25 rise to the top by the metric that matters most for this list: the traditional margin -- the gap between Traditional Score (everything the film gets right about courage, sacrifice, honor, duty, and faith) and Woke Score (everything the film loads in from the progressive ideological playbook). The top 25 span genres from Japanese psychological horror to faith-based historical drama, from Bollywood action epics to intimate British dramas about real people. They have almost nothing in common except this: they trust the audience.

This is the full-year ranking through early October 2026. The midyear edition is now obsolete. Each entry links to the complete VirtueVigil review with methodology breakdowns, content warnings, and parental guidance. Ranked most traditional first.
