We have now reviewed over 715 films and shows at VirtueVigil. Across that entire database, a pattern emerges: some films carry an ideological payload so heavy it overwhelms everything else, craft, performance, storytelling. These are not films with a point of view. Every film has a point of view. These are films where the point of view is the entire product, and the rest is delivery mechanism.

This list ranks the 25 most ideologically aggressive films and shows we have ever scored, from most woke to least woke. Every entry links to the full VirtueVigil review with complete scoring methodology. The margin, tradScore minus wokeScore, reflects how thoroughly progressive ideology dominates the work. A -38 margin means the film's woke content overwhelms its traditional content by 38 points on our 100-point dual scale. These are not close calls.

What makes this list different from genre-specific rankings: these 25 films come from every corner of the industry. Thrillers, horror, animation, musicals, prestige dramas, superhero blockbusters. The ideology crosses genre boundaries effortlessly. What unites them is the same quality: each one argues, explicitly or structurally, that traditional institutions, values, and ways of seeing the world are not just wrong but actively harmful, and each one was celebrated by the institutions that gatekeep cultural legitimacy. Ranked from most woke to least woke.

#1 (Most Woke): Conclave (2024)

STRONGLY WOKE MARGIN: -39 WOKE

Genre: Thriller • Platform: Streaming

The most dangerous kind of progressive film: one that is genuinely excellent. Edward Berger's papal thriller is visually sumptuous, masterfully acted by Ralph Fiennes, and structured as a compulsively watchable political procedural, all in service of a conclusion that treats the election of an intersex pope as divine correction of the Church's sins. The film argues that the Catholic Church's entire institutional architecture is a system of exclusion that must be overturned from within, and that the people most qualified to lead it are the ones the institution has spent centuries rejecting. Every cardinal represents a different corrupt faction, the reactionary, the ambitious careerist, the liberal compromiser, and the one virtuous choice is the one the Church would never make on its own. At -39, it sits alone at the top of our database as the most woke film we have ever reviewed. The craft is undeniable. So is the thesis.

#2: Zootopia 2 (2025)

STRONGLY WOKE MARGIN: -38 WOKE

Genre: Animation • Platform: Theatrical

Disney's animated buddy-cop sequel takes the original's progressive allegory and turns every dial to maximum. Parents who dismissed the first film's messaging as background noise will find no such comfort here. At -38, it is tied for the second-most woke film in our database, and it is marketed to children.
