Parents spend hours checking ratings. PG-13 or R? How much violence? Any nudity? These are the questions we have been trained to ask. They are the wrong questions.

The MPAA rating tells you what is visible on screen. It does not tell you what the film is teaching your child to believe. A PG-13 movie with zero on-screen sex can spend two hours arguing that traditional marriage is a prison. An R-rated horror film can be scary in one way while inserting an entire political worldview between the jump scares. By the time the credits roll, your child has absorbed the message -- and you never knew there was one to watch for.

Our database has tracked 151 films released or streaming in 2026. Of those, more than a third carry a WOKE LEAN or stronger verdict. Six are STRONGLY WOKE. Another eight land squarely in WOKE territory. The ideological content in this year's releases is not hidden in subtext. It is the marquee. Major directors -- Steven Spielberg, Greta Gerwig, Boots Riley, Emerald Fennell, Maggie Gyllenhaal -- have released films this year that wear their politics on every frame. A24 alone distributed five films on this list. The prestige pipeline and the progressive messaging pipeline are now the same pipeline.

The 15 films below are ranked from most woke to least woke, using the same VirtueVigil Wake Score methodology we apply to every review in our database. Each entry includes the woke score, traditional score, verdict, what parents need to know, and a link to the full review for readers who want the complete trope breakdown. This is not about banning art. It is about knowing what your child is being shown -- and deciding, with full information, whether it belongs in your home.
