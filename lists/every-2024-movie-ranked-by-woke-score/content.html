2024 was the year Hollywood's ideological machinery ran at full throttle while audiences increasingly voted with their wallets and their attention spans. It was the year an openly progressive film about papal succession (Conclave) became an awards-season juggernaut. It was the year a body-horror film about female aging (The Substance) became a cultural phenomenon. It was the year Deadpool & Wolverine -- proudly irreverent, proudly un-woke -- made $1.3 billion and embarrassed every pundit who predicted superhero fatigue would kill it. And it was the year Reagan, a $25 million biopic 14 years in the making, found its audience despite near-total critical hostility.

The VirtueVigil Woke Score (VVWS) measures progressive ideological content: identity politics, institutional distrust, progressive sexual framing, and systemic oppression narratives. The Traditional Score measures traditional values: courage, family, faith, sacrifice, moral clarity, and individual agency. The margin between them is what matters. A negative margin means the film pushes progressive ideology. A positive margin means it affirms traditional values. The size of the gap tells you how hard it pushes.

Of the 100 scored 2024 films in our database, 28 lean woke, 35 are roughly balanced, and 37 lean traditional. On raw numbers, that looks surprisingly even. But the distribution of cultural attention was anything but. Conclave, Anora, Emilia Perez, and The Substance dominated awards season. They received the marketing budgets, the magazine covers, the Oscar campaigns, and the cultural conversation. The traditional films -- with the notable exception of Deadpool & Wolverine, which succeeded despite the critical establishment, not because of it -- fought for scraps of attention. The 2024 pattern is the same as every recent year: progressive films get the megaphone; traditional films have to be undeniable just to be heard.

Here is every 2024 film in the VirtueVigil database, ranked from most woke to most traditional. Every entry links to the full review with content warnings for parents and complete category breakdowns.
