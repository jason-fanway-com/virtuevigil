VirtueVigil scored 139 films and shows released in 2025. Across those reviews, a clear pattern emerged: Hollywood still knows how to make movies that honor traditional values. They just don't get the same marketing budget as the films built around progressive ideology. This list is the correction.

Below are the 15 most traditional films and shows of 2025, ranked by VirtueVigil Trad Score margin. The margin is the gap between traditional score and woke score. The larger the gap in the traditional direction, the more the film affirms rather than subverts. Every entry links to the full VirtueVigil review with complete trope breakdowns. If you spent 2025 wondering whether anything worth watching was still being made, this is your answer.

The Takeaway

All 15 films and shows on this list scored STRONGLY TRADITIONAL. That is not a coincidence. 2025 produced genuinely excellent traditionally-scored content across nearly every genre: animated family films (SpongeBob, How to Train Your Dragon, Dog Man), faith-based cinema (David, The Chosen), action blockbusters (Mission: Impossible, Reacher, The Accountant 2), sports comedies (Happy Gilmore 2), martial arts (Karate Kid: Legends, Demon Slayer), true-story dramas (Brave the Dark, Last Breath, Song Sung Blue), and international art cinema (The Blue Trail).

The lesson of 2025 is not that Hollywood has abandoned traditional values. It is that traditional content is being made, often at high quality, but it receives a fraction of the cultural promotion that woke content commands. The SpongeBob Movie nearly doubled the traditional score of the next closest competitor and nobody in entertainment media treated it as noteworthy. Our scoring makes the pattern visible.
