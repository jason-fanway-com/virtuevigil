2026 is the year television completed its reversal. After a decade of prestige-TV-as-progressive-sermon, the numbers are in, and they tell a story the industry did not expect: the shows audiences are actually watching lean traditional. Of the 38 scored 2026 TV seasons in the VirtueVigil database, 14 lean woke, 10 are roughly balanced, and 14 lean traditional. But the distribution of attention matters more than the raw count. The traditional shows (Reacher, Batman: Caped Crusader, Little House on the Prairie, Lioness) are drawing massive audiences. The woke shows are winning awards and media attention, but increasingly, that is all they are winning.

The VirtueVigil Woke Score (VVWS) measures progressive ideological content: identity politics, institutional distrust, progressive sexual framing, and systemic oppression narratives. The Traditional Score measures traditional values: courage, family, faith, sacrifice, moral clarity, and individual agency. The margin between them is what matters. A negative margin means the show pushes progressive ideology. A positive margin means it affirms traditional values.

What follows is the definitive ranking: every 2026 TV season in the VirtueVigil database, ranked from most woke to most traditional. Every entry links to the full review with content warnings for parents and complete category breakdowns.
