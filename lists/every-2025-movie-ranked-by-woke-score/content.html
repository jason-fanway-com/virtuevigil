The woke films -- Snow White, Zootopia 2, Now You See Me: Now You Don't -- underperformed, cratered, or both.

But the cultural machinery that manufactures prestige and awards attention did not adjust. Adolescence was hailed as the year's most important television. The White Lotus continued to dominate the conversation. Eddington and Bugonia earned critical raves. The progressive content pipeline is not slowing down because the box office sends a signal.
